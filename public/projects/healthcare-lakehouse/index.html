Healthcare Lakehouse

Hospitals keep admissions, billing and reference data in separate places, often as yearly extracts. This lakehouse stitches fourteen files together once, validates them, and publishes answers as tables a BI tool can read.

Source code on GitHub ↗

Architecture

What I built

  1. Consolidate

    Combined five years of admission and billing extracts with the patient, doctor, hospital and procedure files.

  2. Validate

    Silver notebooks check nulls, duplicates, business keys, whitespace, allowed values, money and formats. Incomplete records go to quarantine tables instead of being deleted.

  3. Publish

    Five Gold tables answer one manager question each, such as revenue by hospital or admissions by year.

Evidence and technical details

Data sources
  • Clinical (EHR): patients, doctors, and admissions split into yearly files for 2021–2025.
  • Financial: billing, also yearly for 2021–2025.
  • Master data: hospitals and procedures.

A one-time lakehouse_setup notebook selects the health catalog, creates the bronze, silver and gold schemas and the volume the raw CSVs are uploaded to.

Silver: validation

Bronze keeps the files exactly as received (full-refresh Delta tables). Silver is where the data earns trust. Across the Silver notebooks there are seven kinds of check:

  1. Null validation
  2. Duplicate validation
  3. Business-key validation
  4. Whitespace validation
  5. Domain validation (allowed values)
  6. Financial validation (amounts that make sense)
  7. Standardization of formats and codes

Entities are integrated through business keys: admissions link to patients, doctors, hospitals and procedures, and billing links back to admissions.

Gold: business questions

Each Gold table answers one question a hospital manager would actually ask:

Gold tableQuestion it answers
revenue_by_hospitalWhich hospitals generate the most revenue?
revenue_by_stateWhich states generate the most revenue?
revenue_by_specialtyWhich medical specialties generate the most revenue?
admissions_by_yearHow are admissions changing over time?
procedure_performanceWhich procedures are performed most often?
