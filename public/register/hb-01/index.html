HB-01Resolved

Four spellings of open and closed

Harborview Bank

What was wrong

open and CLOSED next to Open and Closed.

Evidence

How it was detected

Distinct count 4; the four counts add to 36,605

Why it matters

Counting open accounts by an exact match would miss 1,302 of them.

What was done

Capitalised each word: Open 25,971, Closed 10,634.

How it was verified

The quality log records the table’s state after its cleaning steps:

36,605 rows. Open 25,971 and Closed 10,634; every open account has an empty close_date; 40 corrected dates flagged.

Every KPI built on it was then reconciled against totals calculated outside Power BI.
