120 customers copied whole

Harborview Bank

What was wrong

Whole customer rows appear twice.

Evidence

How it was detected

Count 20,120, distinct 20,000, unique 19,880

Why it matters

Every account and loan of a duplicated customer would be counted twice in customer-level KPIs.

What was done

Removed duplicates across all columns.

How it was verified

The quality log records the table’s state after its cleaning steps:

20,000 rows. gender has 2 values, income_band 4, region 5 with none blank, email 20,000 unique, birth_date 104 null, signup_date 0 errors. Every home_branch_id matches a branch.
