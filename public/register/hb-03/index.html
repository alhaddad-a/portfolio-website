Eight versions of two values

Harborview Bank

What was wrong

Case and abbreviations: male, FEMALE, M, m, F, f next to Male and Female.

Evidence

How it was detected

Distinct count 8, expected 2

Why it matters

A slicer on gender would show eight options.

What was done

Capitalised each word, then replaced M with Male and F with Female (whole cell).

How it was verified

The quality log records the table’s state after its cleaning steps:

20,000 rows. gender has 2 values, income_band 4, region 5 with none blank, email 20,000 unique, birth_date 104 null, signup_date 0 errors. Every home_branch_id matches a branch.
