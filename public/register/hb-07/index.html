HB-07Resolved

Shouting emails with a trailing space

Harborview Bank

What was wrong

Upper case and a trailing space, always on the same rows.

Evidence

How it was detected

Contains "@MAIL" 1,030; ends with a space 1,030

Why it matters

The same address in two forms breaks uniqueness and any join on email.

What was done

Trimmed and lower-cased.

How it was verified

The quality log records the table’s state after its cleaning steps:

20,000 rows. gender has 2 values, income_band 4, region 5 with none blank, email 20,000 unique, birth_date 104 null, signup_date 0 errors. Every home_branch_id matches a branch.
