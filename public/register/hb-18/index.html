HB-18Reopened

Accounts closed before they were opened

Harborview Bank

What was wrong

40 accounts closed exactly 30 days before they opened. open_date is right: account IDs follow open-date order.

Evidence

How it was detected

True/false check column, 40 true

Why it matters

An account can’t churn before it exists; these rows would distort closure rates.

What was done

Set close_date to open_date + 30 and flagged the rows in close_date_corrected.

Why it was reopened

A later check against the transactions table showed the correction was wrong for most of these accounts. See HB-L2.

Ask the data owner for the missing November and December 2025 customers and correct the 40 account close dates.
