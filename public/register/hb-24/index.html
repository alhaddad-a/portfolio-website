HB-24Resolved

Loan amounts left blank

Harborview Bank

What was wrong

Fifteen loans have no amount — stored as an empty string.

Evidence

How it was detected

15 empty strings

Why it matters

Lending volume by year would be understated, silently.

What was done

Flagged in loan_amount_calculated, then calculated from payment, rate and term with the standard loan formula, rounded to 100. Tested first on loans with a known amount.

How it was verified

The quality log records the table’s state after its cleaning steps:

5,300 loans. Interest rate 0.038 to 0.145, credit score 520 to 850 with 39 unknown, no empty loan amounts (15 calculated and flagged).

02_Data_Quality_Log.md, line 81 ↗

Every KPI built on it was then reconciled against totals calculated outside Power BI.

Lesson recorded in the project“Before filling a missing value with a formula, test the formula on rows where the answer is known.”
