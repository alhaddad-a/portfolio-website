HB-25Resolved

Rates stored as percentages

Harborview Bank

What was wrong

341 rates are written as percentages (9.96) among decimals (0.0996).

341 rows; 339 after removing duplicates.

Evidence

How it was detected

Sorted descending; filtered values not starting with "0."

Why it matters

One column, two scales: the average rate came out at 71.5%.

What was done

Divided by 100 when 1 or higher. Average fell from 71.5% to about 9%.

How it was verified

The quality log records the table’s state after its cleaning steps:

5,300 loans. Interest rate 0.038 to 0.145, credit score 520 to 850 with 39 unknown, no empty loan amounts (15 calculated and flagged).
