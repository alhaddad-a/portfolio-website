HB-28Resolved

Credit scores of 0 and 999

Harborview Bank

What was wrong

Scores outside the 300–850 scale: 0 on 22 loans, 999 on 17.

Evidence

How it was detected

Minimum 0, maximum 999

Why it matters

Credit score was the strongest predictor of default in this analysis; placeholder scores would land in the wrong bands.

What was done

Replaced with null; loans kept and shown as "Unknown" in score bands.

How it was verified

The quality log records the table’s state after its cleaning steps:

5,300 loans. Interest rate 0.038 to 0.145, credit score 520 to 850 with 39 unknown, no empty loan amounts (15 calculated and flagged).
