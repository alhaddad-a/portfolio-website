HB-34Resolved

9,279 transactions listed twice

Harborview Bank

What was wrong

Exact copies of 9,279 transactions.

Evidence

How it was detected

Count 2,329,114, distinct 2,319,835; Keep Duplicates on all columns returned 18,558 rows

Why it matters

Fee income is a sum of transactions; copies inflate it.

What was done

Removed duplicates.

How it was verified

The quality log records the table’s state after its cleaning steps:

2,319,645 rows. 17 merchant categories, 7 channels plus Unknown, all amounts positive, latest date 2025-12-31, no orphans.
