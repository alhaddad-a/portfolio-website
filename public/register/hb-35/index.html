HB-35Resolved

Transactions on accounts that don’t exist

Harborview Bank

What was wrong

Orphans: account IDs in the A9xxxxx range, while real accounts end at A036605.

150 rows (counted after duplicates were removed).

Evidence

How it was detected

Filter on IDs not starting with "A0"

Why it matters

Money with no account can’t be assigned to a customer, segment or region.

What was done

Inner merge with accounts excluded them (35 credits $95,720.53, 101 debits $24,646.13, 14 fees $112.00).

How it was verified

The quality log records the table’s state after its cleaning steps:

2,319,645 rows. 17 merchant categories, 7 channels plus Unknown, all amounts positive, latest date 2025-12-31, no orphans.

Every KPI built on it was then reconciled against totals calculated outside Power BI.
