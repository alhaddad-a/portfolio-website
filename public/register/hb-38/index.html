HB-38Resolved

34 categories that are really 17

Harborview Bank

What was wrong

Lower case and two trailing spaces, on the same rows.

46,503 rows; 46,320 after removing duplicates.

Evidence

How it was detected

34 distinct, exactly 17 doubled

Why it matters

Spending by category would show every category twice.

What was done

Trimmed and capitalised each word; 17 values.

How it was verified

The quality log records the table’s state after its cleaning steps:

2,319,645 rows. 17 merchant categories, 7 channels plus Unknown, all amounts positive, latest date 2025-12-31, no orphans.
