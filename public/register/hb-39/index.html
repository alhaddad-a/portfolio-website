HB-39Resolved

Direction stored twice

Harborview Bank

What was wrong

Some Debit rows carry a negative amount — the direction is in the sign and in txn_type.

17,403 rows; 17,327 after removing duplicates.

Evidence

How it was detected

Filter on values starting with "-": all Debit

Why it matters

Summing debits would partly cancel itself out.

What was done

Absolute value; direction stays in txn_type.

How it was verified

The quality log records the table’s state after its cleaning steps:

2,319,645 rows. 17 merchant categories, 7 channels plus Unknown, all amounts positive, latest date 2025-12-31, no orphans.
