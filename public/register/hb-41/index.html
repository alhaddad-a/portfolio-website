Missing channel stored as an empty string

Harborview Bank

What was wrong

Normal debits and credits with an empty channel.

12,231 rows; 12,183 after removing duplicates.

Evidence

How it was detected

7 channels plus blank; 12,231 blanks

Why it matters

Channel shares would quietly exclude 12,231 transactions.

What was done

Replaced the blank with "Unknown".

How it was verified

The quality log records the table’s state after its cleaning steps:

2,319,645 rows. 17 merchant categories, 7 channels plus Unknown, all amounts positive, latest date 2025-12-31, no orphans.
