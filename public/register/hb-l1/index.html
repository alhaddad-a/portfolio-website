HB-L1Known limitation

The customers who stop arriving in November

Harborview Bank

What was wrong

New customers almost stop after 31 October 2025; account openings stop on 15 December.

7 new customers in November 2025 and 3 in December, against about 250 a month.

Evidence

How it was detected

A sudden break in a flow that was steady for years — treated as a data question before a business finding.

Why it matters

Active and new customers are understated for November and December 2025. Churn, closures, fees and losses are not affected.

What remains, and what I’d do

Kept the data. Customer counts are reported to October 2025, and this is stated on the dashboard, in the memo and in the README.

Ask the data owner for the missing November and December 2025 customers and correct the 40 account close dates.
