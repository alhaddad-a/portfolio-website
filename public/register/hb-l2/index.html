HB-L2Open

My fix for #18 was wrong for most of the 40

Harborview Bank

What was wrong

The correction applied in #18 — close date = open date + 30 — doesn’t survive a check against another table: most of these accounts kept transacting after their new close date.

27 of the 40 corrected accounts have transactions after the new close date (1,436 rows); 12 checking accounts kept paying the fee (239 fees, $1,692).

Evidence

How it was detected

Testing the cleaning assumption against the transactions table.

Why it matters

Very small: 0.1% of accounts. It explains why fee-paying accounts exceed open Mass checking accounts by 0 to 10 a month.

What remains, and what I’d do

Unresolved and still flagged. Recommended fix: set the close date to the end of the month of the last transaction, or treat the account as open if activity continues to December 2025. Every key result was recalculated without the 40 customers: no conclusion changes.

Ask the data owner for the missing November and December 2025 customers and correct the 40 account close dates.
