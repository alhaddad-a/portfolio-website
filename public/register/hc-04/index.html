HC-04Resolved

Money with fifteen decimal places

Healthcare lakehouse

What was wrong

Nearly half of the patient payments carry floating-point noise: 6585.389999999999 instead of 6585.39.

Evidence

How it was detected

Min/max profile and a financial integrity check on the consolidated billing table.

Why it matters

Sums drift by fractions of a cent and equality checks between totals fail for no business reason.

What was done

Both payment columns rounded to two decimals.

Show the PySpark
billing_clean = (
    billing
    .withColumn(
        "insurance_paid",
        F.round(F.col("insurance_paid"), 2)
    )
    .withColumn(
        "patient_paid",
        F.round(F.col("patient_paid"), 2)
    )
)
